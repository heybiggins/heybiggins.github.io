This page is the public call-to-action and opt-in evidence for texts from Gwen — a personal assistant for biggins (sole proprietor). That's a person, not a company. Gwen uses SMS to help coordinate everyday personal tasks: callbacks, reservations, scheduling, and similar errands.

This is not a marketing list and not a blast program. Nobody is added from purchased, rented, or scraped lists. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How people consent

There are two ways someone can opt in. Both require an active yes. There are no pre-checked boxes, no implied consent from a purchase, and no texts until the person has clearly agreed through one of the paths below.

Path 1 — inbound SMS

Someone texts Gwen's SMS number first. That first inbound message is treated as consent to receive replies related to that conversation.

They then get a confirmation that may note: enrolled in texts from Gwen for biggins; message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply; reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel; privacy at https://heybiggins.com/sms-privacy; terms at https://heybiggins.com/sms-terms.

Path 2 — verbal or written permission

biggins (or Gwen for him) asks the person for permission to text them for a specific coordination task — a callback, reservation, scheduling, or errand. Only after they clearly agree does Gwen send texts.

Script example used when asking:

Can Gwen text you at this number about [task]? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP anytime to opt out. Privacy: heybiggins.com/sms-privacy Terms: heybiggins.com/sms-terms.

If they say no, or don't clearly agree, no text is sent. Consent is for that coordination, not a standing marketing subscription.

What we don't do

  • No purchased, rented, or scraped contact lists
  • No marketing blasts or promotional campaigns
  • No pre-checked boxes or bundled “agree to everything” consent
  • No texts before an active yes through Path 1 or Path 2

Cancel or get help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. After you opt out, you shouldn't get more texts from this program unless you text in again or ask to be contacted.
